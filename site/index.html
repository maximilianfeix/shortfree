          How it works

          1. Sweep. Every valid label (a–z, 0–9, inner hyphens) is asked for at the TLD's authoritative name servers. NXDOMAIN means the name is not in the zone.
          2. Verify. 1–3 character candidates are looked up in the registry's RDAP service. 404 = not registered. Names in redemption or pending delete are listed as dropping.
          3. Repeat. A GitHub Action runs daily: 1–3 characters every day, the 1.8 million 4 character names per TLD in seven daily shards.

          "Free" means not registered. Registries may still reserve a name or price it as premium – your registrar shows the final price.
